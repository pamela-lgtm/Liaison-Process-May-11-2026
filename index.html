The Liaison
Process

Linda's role in the new client journey — focused, focused, focused on the human, on-the-ground work she does best. Pamela owns the administrative spine of onboarding (forms, AppFolio setup, lockbox, portal, marketing launch). Linda is the warm face on the ground — property visits, walkthroughs, weekly owner communication through showings and applications, and meetings with current owners who come to view their properties. Each email touchpoint is a content brief Linda writes in her own voice.

The Handoff
Pamela closes, then formally introduces Linda to the owner
Triggered By
PMA Signed
Pamela's role: Once the management agreement is signed, Pamela writes a personal introduction email connecting the owner to Linda. This is the symbolic transition — Pamela closed the relationship, and now Linda will be the white-glove liaison until the property is tenanted.
In-House Actions
  • Pamela sends introduction letter
    Personally written, warm, signals the relationship continuity. Works whether Linda will visit in person (Lake Nona region) or whether a vendor will visit (out-of-region). The letter sets expectations and introduces Linda as the owner's primary contact through tenant placement.
    Pamela
  • Pamela CCs Linda on the introduction
    So Linda has full context and can pick up the relationship without re-introducing herself.
    Pamela
Pamela → Owner · Introduction Letter Email Brief
Personally written by Pamela. Warm, concise, signals continuity. Each one tailored to the specific owner's tone and the relationship Pamela has built during the lead phase.
Should Cover
  • Welcome to Verandah, acknowledgment of the PMA being signed
  • Formal introduction of Linda Lopez as the New Client Liaison — the owner's primary contact through tenant placement
  • Clear team overview: Pamela handles the admin spine — onboarding intake form, AppFolio setup, lockbox coordination, portal activation, and marketing launch communication. Linda is the warm face on the ground — handling the property walkthrough (Lake Nona, or coordinated vendor for out-of-region), then weekly showings updates, feedback themes, application updates, and price-adjustment conversations during marketing. Harvey (Marketing Manager) runs the listing publish & syndication behind the scenes.
  • Heads-up that Linda will reach out within 24 hours — same day if the PMA is signed by 2 PM — with the next step (the New Client Onboarding form)
  • Note that Pamela remains available for strategy conversations
  • Brief mention that Megan and Luz on the Client Experience team will become the ongoing contacts after lease signing
Tone
  • Founder voice — warm, personal, signals the relationship matters
  • Reassuring that the handoff is intentional, not impersonal
  • CC Linda so she has full context

02
Celebration, Onboarding Form & AppFolio Skeleton
Pamela owns — sends celebration page, onboarding form, builds AppFolio skeleton
Owned By
Pamela
Pamela owns this entire phase as Director of New Business Development. The moment the PMA is signed, Pamela sends the celebratory welcome email with a link to the Verandah Welcome & Journey page, sends the New Client Onboarding Jotform, and begins building out the AppFolio skeleton (owner page + property page). Linda enters the picture in Phase 04 (the property visit) where her warmth and on-the-ground presence matter most.
In-House Actions
  • Send celebration email with link to Welcome page
    Confetti landing page that lays out the full new-owner journey, introduces the team, and plants the seed for a 5-star review once the property is leased. Includes preview of the steps Pamela owns and where Linda enters the picture.
    Pamela
  • Send the New Client Onboarding Jotform
    Captures property details Verandah needs to build out AppFolio Property Page. Owner completes — typically 15–20 minutes. Can be embedded in the celebration email or sent as a follow-up.
    Pamela
  • Build AppFolio Owner Page skeleton
    Owner name, phone, email, physical address, temporary tax ID placeholder (999-99-9999 until W-9 received), Tags: source (how they heard about Verandah) and communication preference (per the management agreement). The team fully builds out the page once skeleton is in place.
    Pamela
  • Build AppFolio Property Page skeleton
    Property title, type, address, county, year built, bed/bath count, square footage, reserve amount, management/leasing/renewal fees, late fee %, $500 maintenance limit, bank account assignments. Attach signed PMA + both Jotforms (Pre-Management Prep and New Client Onboarding) to the page. If Lake Nona: add to Lake Nona Property Group.
    Pamela
  • Forward signed PMA to Athenée and Megan
    Internal handoff so both team members have copies of the agreement. Athenée files; Megan reviews for any operational flags.
    Pamela
  • Confirm SureVestor PP Bundle status with Athenée
    Default: property goes into the SureVestor PP Bundle Promo once leased. Occasional exception (e.g., owner declines Platinum, wants Gold only). Pamela confirms with Athenée which path applies for this property.
    Pamela
Pamela → Owner · Celebration & Welcome Email Brief

  • Note that the 5-star Google review request will come post-lease — once they've experienced the full Verandah journey

03
Lockbox Setup
Pamela coordinates with Megan to order the Rently box; Pamela sends owner the guide
Owned By
Pamela
Pamela owns this phase as part of the admin spine. The Rently lockbox is ordered immediately after the onboarding form is submitted — well before the property is rent-ready. This ensures the box is on-site whenever the owner is available to drop in a key, and we're never the bottleneck on access. Megan is available as backup if the owner has technical Rently questions Pamela can't resolve directly.
In-House Actions
  • Pamela asks Megan to order the Rently lockbox
    Quick internal message to Megan with the property address. Megan handles the ordering and arranges installation through one of our vendors.
    Pamela
  • Megan orders box and coordinates vendor install
    Vendor installs the lockbox at the property. Box arrives empty — owner places the key per the access guide.
    Megan
  • Pamela emails owner with Rently access guide
    Heads up that the box is being installed, reassures that no one can enter until they place the key, and links to the full owner guide. Notes Megan is available as backup for any technical Rently questions.
    Pamela
  • Pamela receives written confirmation that the key is in the box
    Non-negotiable checkpoint. Once the owner confirms, Megan tests access from her end and documents in AppFolio.
    Pamela

Rently Lockbox Access Guide
Pamela → Owner · Rently Lockbox Heads-Up Email Brief
Prepares the owner for the lockbox installation and walks them toward placing the key. Should land before the vendor arrives at the property.
Should Cover
  • Acknowledge receipt of the onboarding form — thank you, the team is moving forward
  • Heads-up that a vendor will be installing the Rently lockbox in the coming days at the property
  • Critical reassurance: the box arrives empty — no one can enter until the owner places a key inside
  • Note that the owner does not need to be home for the installation, and does not need to place the key right away
  • Link to the Rently Lockbox Owner Guide
  • Mention that a friend or family member can place the key on the owner's behalf — just provide their phone number so Megan can register them with Rently
  • Request written confirmation (email or text) once the key is placed — this is the formal trigger to test access
  • Note that Megan is available as a backup resource for any technical Rently questions
Tone
  • Reassuring — owners can be uneasy about a lockbox on their door before they've placed the key
  • Make it clear there's no rush — the box will quietly wait at the property
Owner Experience
  • Hears that the lockbox is being installed
    No surprise if they drive by or a neighbor mentions it. Reassured that no one can enter until they place the key.
    Owner
  • Reads the Rently Lockbox Guide
    Linked owner-facing guide explains registration, key placement, and confirmation step.
    Owner
  • Places key in lockbox (or has friend/family do it)
    Single house key only. Other items left on kitchen counter for Linda or vendor to gather during walkthrough.
    Owner
  • Sends written confirmation to Linda
    Email or text confirming the key is in the box.

04
Property Walkthrough
Linda visits in person, or a vendor visits if outside Lake Nona
Triggered By
Owner Schedule
Two paths, same form: If the owner is in the Lake Nona region, Linda visits in person — ideally when the property is rent-ready, or just before. This is where Linda's warmth shines. If the property is outside the region, Megan coordinates a trusted vendor to visit on Verandah's behalf and the vendor reports back to Megan. Either way, the on-site walkthrough form is completed and key items are gathered.
In-House Actions
  • Determine path: Linda visits or Megan schedules vendor
    Lake Nona region → Linda visits in person. Outside region → Megan coordinates a trusted vendor to handle the walkthrough on Verandah's behalf. The form and the data captured are identical regardless of who visits.
    Linda
  • Megan schedules the vendor (out-of-region only)
    For properties outside Lake Nona, Megan reaches out to a trusted vendor and coordinates timing with the owner directly. Linda is informed but does not own the scheduling.
    Megan
  • Schedule Linda's visit (Lake Nona properties)
    Ideal timing: when the owner says the property is rent-ready, or just before. Coordinate around owner's schedule.
    Linda
  • Conduct walkthrough using the on-site form
    The form captures appliances, fixtures, flooring, kitchen/bath finishes, outdoor features, and reference photos. All data feeds the AppFolio Property Page and marketing copy. Linda completes the form herself for Lake Nona walkthroughs; for out-of-region, vendor completes and submits to Megan.
    Linda
  • Gather keys and access items into branded envelope — left at the property
    Verandah-branded key envelope: gathers all garage remotes, gate fobs, mailbox keys, amenity cards, etc. Note: a single house key remains in the Rently lockbox — only the additional items go in the envelope. The envelope is left at the property in a designated, secure spot — chosen so it's out of view and out of reach during prospect tours. These items belong with the property and remain on-site, ready for the tenant at move-in. Linda handles this for Lake Nona walkthroughs; vendor handles for out-of-region.
    Linda
  • Submit completed form & document envelope placement
    Form data flows to the AppFolio Property Page. The key envelope stays at the property in its designated spot, ready for the tenant at move-in. Linda or vendor photographs the envelope in place as part of the walkthrough form for documentation.
    Linda

Walkthrough Tool
On-Site Property Walkthrough Form
Linda → Owner · Walkthrough Scheduling Email Brief
Coordinates timing for the on-site walkthrough — adapted depending on whether Linda visits in person or Megan has scheduled a vendor.
Should Cover
  • Explain the purpose of the walkthrough — capture appliances, fixtures, flooring, finishes for the AppFolio Property Page and listing
  • Note the approximate time required (30–45 minutes)
  • Ideal timing window — when the property is rent-ready, or shortly before
  • For Lake Nona properties: Linda is doing the walkthrough herself; ask for a few times that work and offer to flex around their schedule
  • For out-of-region properties: Megan has scheduled a trusted vendor to handle the walkthrough on Verandah's behalf; the vendor will reach out directly to schedule
  • If property is occupied, ask owner to give tenant the customary notice; if vacant, confirm Rently access is in place
Tone
  • Light and accommodating — Linda flexes to the owner's schedule, not the other way around
  • For vendor path: position it as Verandah's standard for properties outside the service area, not a downgrade
Owner Experience
  • Coordinates walkthrough timing with Linda
    Owner picks a time that works around their schedule.
    Owner
  • Meets Linda (or vendor) at the property — optional
    If the owner is available, the in-person meeting is a chance to walk through the property together and discuss anything specific. If they're not available, the visit can be conducted using Rently access.
    Owner

06
Marketing Launch
Harvey publishes & syndicates the listing; Pamela emails the owner with the live link and overview
Owned By
Harvey + Pamela
Two roles, working in tandem. Once the property is rent-ready, the walkthrough is complete, marketing photos are in hand, and the listing description is written, Harvey (Marketing Manager) publishes the listing across major rental platforms and runs the marketing launch behind the scenes. Pamela then sends the owner the live listing links and a brief overview — and reintroduces Linda as the owner's direct contact for showings, feedback, and applications from this point through tenant placement.
In-House Actions
  • Confirm rent-ready status with the owner
    Property is clean, repairs completed, walkthrough done, marketing materials ready. Owner confirms they're ready for listing to go live.
    Pamela
  • Hand listing description & marketing materials to Harvey
    Pamela writes the listing description in brand voice and forwards to Harvey along with marketing photos and any property-specific notes Harvey needs to know.
    Pamela
  • Publish listing on AppFolio + syndicate to major platforms
    Harvey publishes the listing and triggers syndication across Zillow, Trulia, Apartments.com, and other major rental platforms. Confirms listing is live across all channels and reports back to Pamela with the live links.
    Harvey
  • Email owner with the live listing links & marketing overview
    Celebratory note — your property is live! Includes the AppFolio public-facing URL and the major syndication links (Zillow, Trulia, Apartments.com). Sets expectation that Linda will take over weekly communication from here through tenant placement.
    Pamela
  • Brief Linda on the listing going live
    Internal handoff — Linda is now the owner's primary contact for showings, feedback, and applications.
    Pamela

Through showings, feedback & applications
07
The White-Glove Period
Linda hand-holds the owner through showings, feedback, and applications
Through
Lease Signed
This is the heart of Linda's role. From the moment the property is listed through lease signing, Linda is the owner's voice — keeping them apprised of activity, fielding questions, relaying applications, providing the white-glove experience that justifies Verandah's positioning. If she doesn't know an answer, she always knows who on the team does.
In-House Actions
  • Send weekly owner updates
    Standing cadence — typically a 3-day check-in shortly after listing, then 7-day intervals. Adjusts to owner communication preference. Reports inquiries, showings (Rently self-tours + scheduled), feedback themes, days on market.
    Linda
  • Monitor market signals; flag price reviews
    If days-on-market or showing-to-application conversion lag comparable properties, Linda opens the price conversation with data — proactively, before it becomes urgent.
    Linda
  • Coordinate with internal team on owner questions
    If the owner has a question outside Linda's direct expertise (e.g., maintenance, accounting), she routes it to the right team member and stays in the loop until it's resolved. The owner always hears back from Linda — never the team member directly.
    Linda
  • Receive applications from internal team and update owner
    When an application comes in and is processed by the team, Linda relays the outcome to the owner. Verandah makes approval/denial decisions based on our standards — the owner is informed, not asked to approve individual applicants.
    Linda
  • Document all owner communication in AppFolio
    Every call note, email summary, and text exchange logged so the team has full context.
    Linda
  • Meet with current owners who want to view their property
    A separate workflow that runs in parallel — current Verandah owners (with existing tenants or recently vacated properties) sometimes request property visits. Linda is the warm face who meets them at the property and walks them through. This is part of Linda's ongoing relationship-stewardship role across the Verandah portfolio, not just new clients.
    Linda
Owner Experience
  • Receives steady, predictable updates from Linda
    Never wonders what's happening. Always has a single point of contact. Feels held.
    Owner
  • Can ask Linda anything at any time
    Linda answers what she can and routes the rest — owner only ever talks to Linda.
    Owner
